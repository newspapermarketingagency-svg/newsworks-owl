    How this is calculated, and what it assumes
    • Learners whose next email is one the LMS sends automatically (e.g. "LMS reminder 1 - not started", "LMS Congratulations") are left out of the list below entirely — the "Handled by LMS" count on the summary strip is how many that is. Only emails that need a manual Newsworks send, plus "not activated" and "none due" learners, are shown.
    • The "Recommended email" column shows each email's identifier, not its subject line — subject copy is still going through approval. Every identifier the LMS sends automatically starts with "LMS"; everything Newsworks sends manually starts with "Newsworks", so the two are easy to tell apart once reports are combined.
    • "In progress" deliberately stays one bucket (completed module 1, not yet module 4) rather than splitting by exactly which module someone's on — that would need its own reminder ladder per module, which the schedule keeps out of scope for now. The copy just won't name a specific module.
    • Stage is worked out from each module's status: whether module 1, 4, 5 and 7 are marked Completed, and separately whether the certificate has been downloaded.
    • "Not started" reminders are timed from the Learner Start Date column, which arrived with the new LMS export. If an older-style export is dropped in without that column, this automatically falls back to Created On instead. "In progress" reminders are timed from the module 1 completion date.
    • Where several reminder thresholds have already passed, the tool shows the most recent (highest) one — it doesn't know which reminders have actually been sent.
    • Learners not marked Active are flagged as needing an activation resend, but Newsworks' activation sequence isn't week‑numbered in the schedule, so no specific reminder number is suggested.
    • The Halfway and Nearly‑there emails are one‑off milestones (fired once on completing module 4 / module 5). Completing module 7 fires "LMS Congratulations" straight away; downloading the certificate later fires its own separate "LMS Certificate" email.
    • Once module 7 is complete, two more things are tracked independently and shown by priority: "Newsworks follow up 1" at 4 weeks after completing module 7, and "Newsworks deleting your account" at 50 weeks after the learner's last access across any module (not the completion date) — whichever is due, or overdue, wins.
    • The send checklist lists one row per email identifier currently due, with its own "Download CSV" for just those learners. There's no single .zip of everything, so "Download all as CSVs" instead saves each file in turn — your browser may ask permission after the first couple ("this site wants to download multiple files"); allowing that lets the rest through. Where a saved file genuinely isn't possible, it copies that identifier's rows to the clipboard instead. The checkboxes are just for your own tracking while you work through the list and reset whenever a new report is loaded.
    • Nothing here sends anything — it's a read‑only view to help you decide what to send and to whom.
